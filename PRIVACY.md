# Privacy Policy — VÄIN

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

VÄIN ("the App") is developed for the "Radio Tallinn 1965" project, run by
the Estonian Centre for Contemporary Music (ECCM). This policy explains what
information the App collects, how it is used, and who to contact about it.

## Who is responsible for your data

Estonian Centre for Contemporary Music (ECCM)
Contact: Tarmo Johannes — trmjhnns@gmail.com

## Information the App collects

### Push notifications
To notify you about new broadcasts, articles, and other events, the App
registers your device with Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM), a service
provided by Google, and sends the resulting device token to our server so
we can deliver notifications to it. This token is only used to send you
notifications about content in the App and is not shared with any other
third party or used for advertising.

### App preferences
Settings such as your preferred server, whether you've dismissed the
startup info dialog, and (if you broadcast) your last-used broadcast
title/description/channel are stored locally on your device. This
information never leaves your device.

### Becoming a Contributor
The App, and the companion web page at eccm.ee/radio1965/contributor, let
you register as a permanent "Contributor" in order to publish content or
broadcast audio under your own name. Registering asks for a name, an
e-mail address, and a password, each used for a specific, limited purpose:

- **Name.** Stored on our server and shown publicly next to anything you
  publish (for example, as "Shared by: &lt;name&gt;" on a shared item), so
  that other users know who shared it. We don't use it for anything else.
- **E-mail address.** Stored on our server and used only for
  account-related messages that are necessary to operate your account:
  confirming your registration, and - only if you ask for it - resetting a
  forgotten password. We do not use your e-mail for newsletters, marketing,
  or any other purpose, and we do not sell, rent, or otherwise distribute
  it to third parties. The only party that ever sees it besides us is the
  mail server that delivers those two messages on our behalf.
- **Password.** Stored on our server only as a one-way cryptographic hash
  (bcrypt). We cannot read, recover, or view your actual password at any
  time - not even internally.

A separate, temporary "Contributor" role also exists, requiring only a
shared password with no name or e-mail collected at all - see "App
preferences" above; that information stays only on your device and is
covered by that section instead.

#### Deleting your Contributor data
You can delete your registration data at any time from the App's menu:
**Leave Contributor role → Delete my data**. Doing so permanently removes
your e-mail address, password, and login credentials from our server - the
account can no longer log in or receive e-mails afterward. Your name is
kept attached to anything you had already published before deleting your
data, so that content keeps crediting the right person, but it is no
longer linked to any e-mail address, password, or login once you delete
your data this way. If you'd instead like your name removed from
already-published content as well, or have any other request about your
data, contact us directly (see "Contact" below).

### Microphone access
If you use the App's broadcasting feature, the App accesses your device's
microphone in order to stream your audio live to the selected channel.
Audio is only captured while you are actively broadcasting, and (if you
choose to enable "Save stream") the resulting recording is stored on our
server as part of the published broadcast. Microphone audio is never
otherwise recorded, analyzed, or shared.

### Server communication
The App communicates with our server to fetch the list of available events,
articles, and streams, and to load web content within the App. Standard
technical information (such as your IP address) may be logged by our server
or hosting provider as part of normal web server operation, for security
and troubleshooting purposes only.

## What we do not do

- We do not use the data described above for advertising or profiling.
- We do not sell, rent, or otherwise distribute your personal data,
  including your Contributor name and e-mail address, to third parties.
- We do not use your Contributor e-mail address for marketing or
  newsletters - only for the account-related messages described above.
- We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs beyond the push
  notification service (Firebase Cloud Messaging) described above.

## Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children, and Contributor registration is not
intended for children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App's features change. We will update the
"Last updated" date whenever this policy changes.
