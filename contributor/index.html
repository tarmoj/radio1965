Terms of Use

VÄIN has zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior, by contributors or listeners alike.

By continuing, you agree not to post content that is illegal, hateful, harassing, sexually explicit, or otherwise objectionable, and not to abuse or harass other users.

Any listener can also block an author at any time from within the app. Blocking hides that author's posts from your feed immediately and notifies the VÄIN team for review.

By continuing you confirm that you have read and accept these terms.
